Define and discuss the frames

CorRes found the frames in your corpus. Here you work out what each one means: see what the documents show about it, read a drafted definition, and refine it in conversation until it says what you have found.

1The analysis from CorRes

pluralitie works on a finished CorRes analysis. Nothing is uploaded again: it reads the analysis where CorRes left it.

Have an analysis file from the earlier CorRes? Name its frames here.

2What the documents show

Counted from your matrix, frame by frame: the concepts that mark each frame out, how many documents carry it, which concepts travel together, and where the frames meet. No model is involved in this step.

3Draft the definitions

A language model reads the counts above and drafts, for each frame, a working name, a definition, a discussion of the evidence, and questions to take to your documents. It has not read the documents: you have. Treat every draft as a starting point.

Reading the evidence and drafting…

4Discuss and refine

Take each frame in turn. Read the documents it suggests, then say what you found: agree, disagree, or ask. Where the conversation gives reason to change a definition, a revision is proposed and you decide. You can also rewrite any definition yourself.

5Take it with you

The report carries each frame's working definition, the evidence it rests on, the documents to read first, every earlier definition, and the whole conversation.
