Correlating Resonance · pluralitie

Define and discuss the frames

CorRes found the frames in your corpus. Here you work out what sets each one apart, and where they pull against each other. See what the documents show, read a drafted definition, and refine it in conversation until it says what you found.

1The analysis from CorRes

pluralitie starts from a finished CorRes analysis. Nothing is uploaded again: it reads the analysis where CorRes left it.

2What the documents show

Counted from your matrix, frame by frame: the concepts that set each frame apart, how many documents carry it, which concepts travel together, and where the frames meet. No model is used in this step. A concept appearing in a document is not that document's position on the issue.

3Draft the definitions

A language model reads the counts above and drafts, for each frame, a working name, a definition, a discussion of the evidence, and questions to take to your documents. It has not read the documents: you have. Treat every draft as a starting point.

Reading the evidence and drafting…

4Discuss and refine

Take each frame in turn. Read the documents it suggests, then say what you found: agree, disagree, or ask. Where the conversation gives reason to change a definition, a revision is proposed and you decide. You can also rewrite any definition yourself.

5Take it with you

The report carries each frame's working definition, the evidence it rests on, the documents to read first, every earlier definition and the whole conversation. The frames stay provisional until you run the reviewer's checks in CorRes.
