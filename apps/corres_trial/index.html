corres v3.0: the trial

CorRes identifies concepts that co-occur beyond what their frequencies predict, groups them by their reproducibility across independent halves of the corpus, reads each group for the concepts that distinguish it and the populations that voice it, and reports every result against references computed by permuting the corpus itself; it certifies no frame.
